Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

General Questions

What are the library's hours of operation?
The Maplebrook Public Library is open Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM, and Saturday from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays.
How can I get a library card?
You can apply for a library card in person at the library or online through our website. Please bring a valid ID and proof of address.
Are there any late fees for overdue books?
Yes, there are late fees for overdue books. The fee is $0.25 per day for regular books and $1.00 per day for DVDs and special collections.
